Privacy Policy for Echo Tower
Effective Date: April 15, 2026


1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile application Echo Tower, created by Taldius. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users and ensuring a safe gaming experience.

2. Information Collection and Use

Echo Tower does not collect, store, or share any personal data or usage statistics. No analytics tools (like Firebase) or advertising SDKs (like AdMob) are integrated into the application.

However, the app is distributed via the Google Play Store, which uses Google Play Services for core functionality. These services may collect certain information as governed by Google's own privacy policy.

3. Log Data

In the event of a critical error or crash, Google may collect "Log Data" through the Android system to help identify the problem. This information is handled by Google and is not directly stored by Taldius.

4. Children’s Privacy

Echo Tower does not collect any personally identifiable information. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since no data is collected, the game is safe for players of all ages.

5. Security

Even though the app does not collect personal data, we strive to ensure that the application itself is secure and reliable for all users.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Taldius may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about the Privacy Policy of Echo Tower, please contact Taldius at: contact@taldius.com
